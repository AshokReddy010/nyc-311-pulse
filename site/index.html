How this works

Data. NYC Open Data's 311 Service Requests (2020 to present), queried every morning through the Socrata API. Daily counts per complaint type and borough are aggregated on the server, so only small tables travel.

Late arrivals. Requests keep arriving for days, so the last ten days are re-pulled on every run, and a day that is still far below its usual volume is not shown as the headline.

Unusual. A robust z-score against the same weekday over the previous eight weeks (median and median absolute deviation). Types with fewer than 15 requests on a typical day are left out.

Closure. Measured on requests at least eight days old, so "within a week" is final. Closed dates earlier than the created date, a known data artefact, are treated as unknown. "Closed" means the agency closed the request, not necessarily that the problem was fixed.
